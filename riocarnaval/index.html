01The journey

City heat.
Forest rain.

This journey moves to Brazil’s unmistakable rhythm: from Copacabana mornings and Sambadrome nights to the quiet drama of jungle waterways. The contrast is electric, the color constant, and every day opens onto a new world.

02Day by day

Your itinerary

Nine days,
two worlds.

Day 01February 10

Touch down in Rio

Arrive in Rio de Janeiro in the morning, settle into beachfront Copacabana, and begin the journey over an open-fire Brazilian feast.

  • Morning arrival in Rio de Janeiro
  • Check in for five nights at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro
  • Dinner at Assador, a Brazilian churrasco restaurant
Day 02February 11

Icons by day, Rio after dark

Meet Rio through its great landmarks, from Christ the Redeemer to Sugarloaf Mountain, before seeing where the city’s night leads.

  • Rio de Janeiro city tour
  • Christ the Redeemer
  • Escadaria Selarón
  • Sugarloaf Mountain
  • Explore Rio nightlife
Day 03February 12

A day at the water’s edge

Leave the day beautifully unhurried: Copacabana’s sweep of sand, Ipanema’s easy rhythm, and the Atlantic in every direction.

  • Beach day at Copacabana Beach or Ipanema Beach
Day 04February 13

Carnival at the Sambadrome

A day of color, music, and anticipation builds toward an all-night celebration inside Rio’s legendary Sambadrome.

  • Lunch in the Leblon neighborhood
  • Pick up Folia Tropical gear
  • 10:00 PM departure for the Sambadrome
  • 10:00 PM–9:00 AM celebration in the Camarote 8 Folia Tropical section
  • All drinks and meals included
Day 05February 14

A slower morning in Copacabana

After Carnival’s crescendo, the morning begins late and the day unfolds at an easy Rio pace.

  • Late brunch in Copacabana
  • Afternoon at leisure
Day 06February 15

North to Manaus

Trade the Atlantic coast for the gateway to the Amazon, with Manaus as the home base for the final chapter.

  • Gol Airlines flight
  • Depart Rio at 7:55 AM; arrive Manaus at 11:00 AM
  • Check in for three nights at Tropical Hotel da Amazonia, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel
Day 07February 16

Into the Amazon

A private day in one of the world’s most biodiverse landscapes, where the forest canopy, waterways, and wildlife set the pace.

  • Private Amazon jungle tour
  • Rainforest and wildlife encounters
Day 08February 17

Where two rivers meet

Travel by boat to the remarkable seam where the dark Rio Negro and sandy Solimões run side by side before becoming one.

  • Half-day excursion to the Meeting of the Waters
  • Boat journey from Manaus
  • Tours may also include flooded forest or giant water lilies
Day 09February 18

The journey home

Depart Manaus carrying two very different visions of Brazil: Rio in celebration and the Amazon in living color.

  • Check out and depart for home
