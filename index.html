A slower, more scenic way to experience Yosemite.

This itinerary balances Yosemite’s classic viewpoints with time to unwind at Rush Creek Lodge. Saturday is built around a relaxed Cathedral Beach picnic, followed by easy valley stops and an earlier dinner back at the lodge.

Cathedral Beach
Merced River · Yosemite Valley

Featured experience

Cathedral Beach, unhurried.

Plan for roughly three hours, with enough time for a shaded picnic, river views and family photos without rushing.

  1. 11:00 AM
    Arrive & choose a shaded picnic area

    Settle near the river while keeping a respectful distance from sensitive vegetation.

  2. 11:30 AM
    Picnic lunch

    Enjoy views toward El Capitan and Cathedral Rocks.

  3. 12:30 PM
    Riverfront stroll & photos

    Walk the shoreline, look for reflections and take relaxed group photos.

  4. 1:15 PM
    Optional nearby viewpoint

    Continue to Sentinel Bridge or another nearby valley stop, depending on parking and energy levels.

  5. 2:00 PM
    Depart for the next valley stop
River safety: conditions can change quickly. Enter the water only where permitted and only when currents and water levels are safe.
