The alpine edit

What to wear

01

Start with breathable layers

A long-sleeve base layer and light sweater make it easy to adjust between the car, valleys, and exposed viewpoints.

02

Bring summit warmth

Pack a lightweight insulated jacket, warm hat, and gloves. Sulphur Mountain and the Icefields can feel much colder than Canmore.

03

Prepare for mountain weather

Carry a waterproof shell and wear sturdy, water-resistant walking shoes with reliable grip.

04

Keep the alpine sun in mind

Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a refillable water bottle belong in the day bag—even on a cool or cloudy morning.
